HEARTHSIDE COMMUNITY FUND - DONOR RETENTION FINDINGS
Spring 2024 | 3 consultants | Pro bono engagement
(Fictional case written for the sample data set. Hearthside Community Fund is not a real nonprofit.)

BACKGROUND
Hearthside is a community foundation that funds food security and youth programmes in a mid-sized city.
Individual giving has grown, but only 41% of first-time donors give a second gift within 18 months.
The development director asked us to find out why and what a small team can do about it.

WHAT WE LOOKED AT
- Eight years of donation records (22,400 donors, 61,000 gifts)
- 24 donor interviews (12 retained, 12 lapsed)
- The board's fundraising calendar and the thank-you process

FINDINGS
1. The second gift is the whole game. Donors who gave twice within 18 months were retained at 84% over
   the following five years. First-time donors who heard nothing for 90 days almost never returned.
2. Thank-you timing matters more than content. Gifts acknowledged within 48 hours had a 12-point higher
   second-gift rate than gifts acknowledged after a week, regardless of letter quality.
3. Monthly giving is under-used. Only 6% of donors give monthly, but they retain at 91% and give 2.3 times
   more per year. Few donors were ever asked.
4. Event donors behave differently from online donors. Gala attendees give once and rarely again unless
   someone follows up personally.
5. Board members want to help but do not know what to say. Three of five said they would make
   donor thank-you calls if given a script.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Automate a 48-hour acknowledgement and a 30-day impact update for every first-time donor.
- Add a monthly-giving ask to the donation page and the thank-you email.
- Create a board "thank-a-thon" twice a year with a call list sorted by gift date.
- Track second-gift rate as the headline retention metric on a one-page dashboard.

IMPACT ESTIMATE
Lifting the second-gift rate from 41% to 50% adds roughly 70 retained donors a year. At the current
average annual gift of $185, that is about $13,000 in year one and more as retained donors compound.

NOTE FOR FUTURE TEAMS
Nonprofit data is messy. Budget two weeks to deduplicate donors before analysis, and agree on one
definition of "lapsed" with the client on day one.
