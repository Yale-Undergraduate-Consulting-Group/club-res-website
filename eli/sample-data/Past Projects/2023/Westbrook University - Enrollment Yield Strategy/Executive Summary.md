# Westbrook University: Enrollment Yield Strategy

**Term:** Fall 2023 | **Team:** 4 consultants | **Industry:** Higher education

> Fictional case written for the sample data set. Westbrook University is not a real institution.

## Situation

Westbrook, a private university of 6,200 undergraduates, admitted 8,900 students for a class target of
1,550 but enrolled only 1,380. Yield (the share of admitted students who enroll) had fallen from 19% to
15.5% in four years, and the gap was being closed with deeper financial aid discounting.

## Questions we answered

1. Where in the funnel are admitted students deciding against Westbrook?
2. Which admitted students are most likely to enroll with a modest nudge?
3. Can the university hold its discount rate while still reaching the class target?

## Approach

- Survey of 1,100 admitted students who declined, plus 40 phone interviews.
- Logistic regression on four admit cycles to identify drivers of yield.
- Review of the communication calendar, campus event attendance and financial-aid award letters.

## Findings

- **Campus visits are the strongest signal.** Admitted students who visited campus before April 1 enrolled
  at 31%, versus 9% for those who did not. Visits were not evenly distributed: students beyond 300 miles
  rarely came.
- **Award letters confused families.** Net cost after grants was hard to find, and 38% of decliners said a
  peer school "seemed cheaper" even when Westbrook's net price was lower.
- **Summer melt is real.** 6% of deposited students never arrived, most often after a missed housing
  or immunisation deadline.
- **Staff time is spent on the wrong students.** Counsellors spent most outreach hours on very high and
  very low likelihood admits, where the nudge effect is smallest.

## Recommendations

1. Launch a virtual admit-day and travel grants for students beyond 300 miles.
2. Redesign the award letter around a single "what you will pay" number.
3. Score admits by yield likelihood and focus counsellor time on the middle 40%.
4. Add a text-message nudge series between deposit and orientation to reduce melt.

## Result to date

The redesigned award letter and the text-message series were piloted for the following admit cycle.
Yield rose to 17.1% with a flat discount rate.
