# Acme Chat: Go-To-Market Strategy for the Mid-Market

**Term:** Spring 2023 | **Team:** 6 consultants, 1 alumni mentor | **Industry:** B2B SaaS, team messaging

> Fictional case written for the sample data set. Acme Chat is not a real company.

## Situation

Acme Chat is a Slack-like team messaging platform with strong adoption in startups and developer teams.
It wanted to move up-market into companies with 200 to 1,000 employees, where it faced entrenched
incumbents, security reviews and procurement cycles. Growth had come almost entirely from free teams
inviting colleagues, and the sales team was only six people.

## Core question

How should Acme Chat take its chat platform to market in the mid-market, and what mix of product-led
growth and sales-led motion gets it to $20M of new annual recurring revenue within 24 months?

## Approach

- 31 customer and prospect interviews, plus 12 win/loss calls.
- Competitive teardown of four messaging and collaboration vendors across features, packaging and positioning.
- Bottom-up market sizing from firmographic data (company size, industry, remote-work share).
- Channel economics model comparing self-serve, inside sales, and partner-led motions.

## Key findings

- **Teams, not companies, are the buying unit.** Deals started when three or more teams were active and a
  single admin asked for single sign-on.
- **Bottom-up seat expansion is the engine.** Accounts that crossed 40 active seats in 90 days converted to
  paid at four times the baseline rate.
- **Security review is where deals stall.** Median delay was 38 days, caused by missing audit logs and
  data-residency answers, not by price.
- **Integrations drive stickiness.** Workspaces with five or more integrations churned at half the rate.

## Recommendations

1. Keep self-serve as the front door, and add a sales-assist layer triggered by usage signals
   (seat count, number of teams, SSO page views).
2. Ship an "IT admin" bundle: SSO, audit logs, retention controls and a security questionnaire pack.
3. Invest in an integrations marketplace and a partner programme with three system integrators.
4. Reposition from "faster than email" to "the place where work gets decided."

## Next steps

A 90-day launch plan is in the final presentation. Acme Chat returned in 2025 for a follow-on engagement
on enterprise pricing and packaging.
