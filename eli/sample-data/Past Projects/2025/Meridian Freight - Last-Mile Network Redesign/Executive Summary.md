# Meridian Freight: Last-Mile Network Redesign

**Term:** Spring 2025 | **Team:** 5 consultants, 1 operations advisor | **Industry:** Logistics

> Fictional case written for the sample data set. Meridian Freight is not a real company.

## Situation

Meridian Freight delivers parcels and bulky goods for e-commerce retailers across the Midwest from
seven regional hubs. Cost per stop had risen 14% in two years, on-time delivery had dropped to 91%, and
two large retail customers threatened to move volume to a competitor with next-day coverage.

## Approach

- Geocoded 2.6 million deliveries and clustered them by route density.
- Built a network model to compare the current hub-and-spoke design with added micro-fulfilment nodes.
- Ride-alongs with 14 drivers and time-and-motion study at two hubs.
- Interviews with dispatchers, hub managers and three retail customers.

## Findings

- **A third of routes are sparse.** 34% of routes average fewer than 55 stops per driver-day, and these routes
  account for 52% of excess cost.
- **Failed first attempts are the hidden cost.** 8.5% of stops need a second visit, mostly because of
  access problems at apartment buildings and no delivery window communicated to the recipient.
- **Hub dwell time is avoidable.** Trucks wait an average of 47 minutes to unload at the two busiest
  hubs because inbound appointments are not scheduled.
- **Customers value reliability over speed.** All three retailers ranked a two-hour delivery window above
  a faster promise.

## Recommendations

1. Consolidate the 12 sparsest routes into a shared-zone model with two partner carriers.
2. Add three micro-fulfilment nodes in the densest metro areas to cut line-haul distance.
3. Introduce delivery windows and recipient notifications to bring the failed-attempt rate below 5%.
4. Schedule inbound appointments at hubs and incentivise early unloading.

## Impact

Network model projects an 11% reduction in cost per stop and on-time delivery above 96% within nine
months. The payback on the micro-fulfilment investment is 22 months in the base case.
