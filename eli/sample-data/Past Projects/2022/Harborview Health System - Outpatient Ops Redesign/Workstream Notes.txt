HARBORVIEW OUTPATIENT OPS - WORKSTREAM NOTES (team working file)

Week 3 interview themes (clinic managers, n=9)
- "We have the physicians, we do not have the slots." Seven of nine managers said templates had not
  been touched since the electronic record migration.
- Front-desk staff spend roughly a quarter of the day on phone tag with patients who miss callbacks.
- Two clinics already run an informal waitlist on a shared spreadsheet. It works, and nobody owns it.

Data caveats
- Scheduling export does not distinguish patient cancellations from clinic-initiated reschedules.
  We treated cancellations inside 24 hours as no-shows, which likely overstates the 17% figure by
  one or two points.
- Visit-type codes changed in 2020. We mapped old codes by hand with the clinic leads.

Open questions for the client
1. Who has authority to change templates? Today it takes three sign-offs.
2. Can the portal intake form be edited without a vendor change request?
3. Is there appetite for nurse-led visits for routine follow-ups?

Team working agreements
- Interview notes go in the shared folder within 24 hours.
- Numbers in slides must trace to a named query. No orphan statistics.
